So, what exactly is anime?

Japanese animation, or known better as anime, is a style of animation that focuses on bright and distinct colors, along with a storytelling narrative representative of Japanese culture.

Over the last few years, anime has become widespread all around the world, quickly capturing the hearts of many fans. Its widespread popularity has led its media to be translated to other languages and spread across many different genres, such as being adapted into game titles.
